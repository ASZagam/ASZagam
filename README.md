### AI Engineer | NLP & Speech AI | Low-Resource Language Technology

I am an AI Engineer with a background in **Computer Engineering**, interested in building intelligent systems that solve real-world problems.

My strongest interest is in **Natural Language Processing and Speech AI**, particularly for **Hausa and other underrepresented African languages**.

I started working with Hausa because it is my native language, and I quickly realized how difficult it is to build modern AI systems for languages with limited datasets, models, and computing resources. That experience moved my interest from simply using AI models to understanding how we can make AI work better for languages that are often left behind.

## 🧠 My Research Direction

> **Making AI more inclusive for Hausa and other African languages.**

A large part of modern AI research is driven by languages with enormous amounts of data and computational resources. I want to explore how similar capabilities can be developed for languages that do not have the same advantages.

My long-term interests are around:

**Low-resource NLP → Multilingual AI → Speech Technology → LLMs → African Language AI**

I want to investigate better ways of learning from limited data, adapting multilingual models, creating useful speech resources, and building AI systems that understand and generate African languages.
